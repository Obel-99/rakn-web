Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

The short version. RAKN stores your account and your training data so the app works and your data follows you between devices. That's it.

No ads. No analytics. No tracking. We don't sell or share your data. Delete your account in the app and your data is gone.

Who we are

RAKN is a training app made in Denmark. We are the data controller for the personal data described here. Questions or requests: rakn.nordic@gmail.com

What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address.
  • Your password, if you sign up with email. It is stored only in hashed form. We can never see it.
  • If you use Sign in with Apple: an anonymous Apple user identifier and the email Apple shares with us. This can be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email". We don't store your name.

Your training data, meaning what you choose to enter:

  • Workouts and exercises you create, with sets, reps, weights and rest times.
  • Completed sessions: date, duration, distances, times, effort rating and any notes you write.
  • Planned sessions in your calendar.
  • Body weight entries, if you choose to log them.

Body weight and training history can say something about your health. We only store it because you enter it, and only use it to show it back to you.

Stored only on your phone, never sent to us: your app settings (theme, sound and vibration), custom exercises, your weekly template, and a temporary copy of an active workout so you can recover it if the app closes.

What we don't collect

  • No location or GPS data.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No advertising identifiers, analytics, or third-party tracking.
  • No data from Apple Health or other apps.

Rest timer alerts are local notifications created on your phone. No push service is involved.

Why we use it

Only to run the app for you: to log you in, save your training, and sync it between your devices. The legal basis is our agreement with you when you create an account (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). For body weight and other health-related entries, your explicit consent, which you give by choosing to enter them and can withdraw by deleting them (Art. 9(2)(a)).

We don't use your data for marketing, profiling or automated decisions, and we don't sell or share it with anyone.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored on your phone and synced to our database, hosted by Supabase on servers in the EU (Ireland). Supabase also handles sign-in. Supabase processes the data only on our behalf, under a data processing agreement. In the rare case that Supabase support staff outside the EU/EEA need access, that is protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and each account can only access its own data.

How long we keep it

For as long as you have an account. You can delete individual workouts, sessions and entries at any time.

Deleting your account: in the app, go to Settings and choose Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all training data linked to it from our servers. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you delete the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, and object to or restrict how we use it. Email rakn.nordic@gmail.com and we'll answer within 30 days.

You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

Children

RAKN is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is used, we'll tell you in the app before it applies.
